# Document templates

Copy only the template needed for the work. Remove prompts when filling it out.

## Proposal

Title, owner, date, status; problem and audience; scope and non-goals; options and tradeoffs; recommendation; evidence; open questions; acceptance needed.

## Decision record

Stable ID and title; owner, date, status; context; decision and approval reference; alternatives; consequences; conditions for revisiting; successor link if superseded.

## Experiment

Question; hypothesis and falsifying result; environment and commit; workload and seed; exact commands; observations and evidence location; uncertainty; conclusion and next action.

## Procedure

Purpose; prerequisites and permissions; ordered steps; expected result; failure handling; rollback or recovery; last verified version and evidence.

## Specification

Purpose and audience; status and owner; terminology; inputs and outputs; normal behavior; invariants; failure behavior; acceptance scenarios; non-goals and open decisions.
